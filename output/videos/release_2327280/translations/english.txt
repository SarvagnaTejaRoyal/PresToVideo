Recounting his February-March 1993 visit to invite Swami Chinmayananda to Chicago for the centenary of the Parliament of the World's Religions,marking 100 years of Swami Vivekananda’s historic address,the Prime Minister noted how Gurudev tirelessly rallied people across America for the event before passing away in San Diego on August 3. To instill national self-respect, Swamiji launched the Gita Gyana Yagna to bring the Upanishads, Vedanta, and scriptures to the masses, resulting today in lakhs of Chinmaya Mission youth passing on Indian values as cultural brand ambassadors."This contribution of Revered Gurudev is an important chapter in India's cultural and spiritual renaissance,” affirmed PM Modi. Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the Chinmaya Amrit Mahotsav Global Sammelan today at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi.
