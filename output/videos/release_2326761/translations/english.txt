The President of India, Smt Droupadi Murmu graced ‘Seva Samman Parv’ and ‘Yuva Prabodhan’ on the occasion of the Nuakhai festival in Raipur, Chhattisgarh today (October 22, 2026). Speaking on the occasion, the President said that Nuakhai Milan Samaroh is not merely a cultural celebration; it is a tribute to our rich traditions, social harmony and unity. Such occasions provide us with an opportunity to reconnect with our roots, take pride in our cultural heritage, and pass on the life values to future generations.
